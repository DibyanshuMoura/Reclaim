What happens next

When a found-item report matches yours, both parties get an email with a private match link. You each decide independently whether to confirm — nothing, not even the other side's contact details, is shared until you both confirm.

No accounts — anonymous reporting. Your email is never published, and free-text location details are never shown to the other party.
