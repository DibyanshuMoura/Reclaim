How it works

Three steps, all private by default

1

Report

Lost and found reports are anonymous. Each report gets a private, one-time link — save it, it is your only way back.

2

Match

The system compares your report against the other side using item type, colors, place, and time. Strong pairs are notified by email.

3

Confirm

Both parties review the match privately. Contact details are shared only after both confirm — or either side can decline.

Under the hood

Built to be honest, not magical

AI with visible work

Photo attributes are extracted with computer vision and displayed to you exactly as they were read — including what the AI was unsure about. They are signals, never proof.

Explainable matching

Every potential match shows the factors behind its score: which details agreed, which conflicted, and by how much. No black box.

Privacy that is enforced

Free-text descriptions of the other party are never shown, tokens grant access only to your own report, and email templates cannot leak counterparty details.

What Reclaim stores — and what it never stores

Stored: report details you type, attributes extracted from the found photo, match scores with their factors, and contact emails used only for match notifications. Photos are deleted once attributes are saved.

Never stored or shown: the other party's free-text location details, their photo, or their identity before mutual confirmation. Report tokens are shown once and stored only as hashes.

Reclaim is a simple, privacy-conscious item-recovery service. If you found something, one photo starts the process; if you lost something, a short description is all it takes.
